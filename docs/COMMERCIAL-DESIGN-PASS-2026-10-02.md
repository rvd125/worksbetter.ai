# Works Better commercial and design pass — 2 October 2026

## Decision and limits

The first release established functional quality and a usable baseline. It did not establish exceptional lead generation, ranking, conversion uplift or a “top 1%” position. A top-percentile claim would need a defined comparison and outcome evidence. This second pass responds to the owner’s request for a more demanding commercial, visual and journey review. The reviewed improvements are staged for controlled release; the dated production supplement will establish what actually shipped.

The core offer remains AI workflows and tools built around the apps and systems a team already knows, across automatic work, human approvals and team-operated tools. Scoping establishes connection/access limits and any changes. Renzo personally designs and builds the solution; fees and scope are agreed before paid work. No price, duration, universal compatibility, saving, testimonial, client name or outcome was invented.

The six full interactive examples, confirmed safeguards explorer, paid-next-step guidance, enquiry/privacy handling and original AI tool remain. The page still has considerable optional depth: early conversion and clearer hierarchy do not prove every section deserves equal attention. Further pruning should respond to genuine visitor behavior and buyer feedback rather than an unsupported percentage claim.
